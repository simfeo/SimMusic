## Short description (80 max)

Play your music by folders. Clean player, letter bar, widget and Android Auto.

## Full description (4000 max)

Sim Music is a music player for the songs on your phone, built around one thing many players do poorly: playing your music by folders.

PLAY BY FOLDERS
• Every music folder in one list, shown by its own name with its path and album art
• One tap plays a whole folder; open it to start from any track
• Prefer a tree? Switch to folder tree view and play a folder together with its subfolders
• Sort folders by name or by full path

FIND MUSIC FAST
• Tracks, Albums, Artists and Folders on a bottom bar, right under your thumb
• Letter bar: drag along the side to jump through your library, with a big letter bubble and haptic ticks
• Your language's alphabet, and natural sorting that ignores case and accents and puts "Track 2" before "Track 10"
• Search across tracks, albums and artists

A CLEAN PLAYER
• Mini player above the tabs and a full player with seek bar, shuffle, repeat and queue
• Swipe the album art to change track, swipe down to close
• Background playback with notification and lock screen controls
• Bluetooth and headset buttons work, and music pauses when headphones disconnect
• Picks up where you left off

SELECT AND MANAGE
• Long-press to select tracks, folders, albums or artists, then play, share or delete them
• Track menu: details (format, bit rate, size, location), go to album or artist, share, set as ringtone, delete

HOME SCREEN WIDGET
• Album art, title and artist with shuffle, previous, play, next and repeat
• Make it taller to see what plays next and jump to any track

ANDROID AUTO
• Browse folders, albums, artists and tracks in your car, with album art
• Search with the car keyboard when parked, or ask by voice
• Shuffle and repeat right on the car screen

MAKE IT YOURS
• Eight accent colors, including Mono
• Follows your phone's light or dark theme
• Available in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Ukrainian and Chinese

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Sim Music has no ads, no account, no internet access and no tracking. Your music and settings never leave your phone.

Sim Music plays the audio files stored on your device. It does not stream music.
