# Sim Music Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Sim Music is a music player for Android, developed by an independent developer (Sim).

## Data collection

Sim Music does not collect, store remotely, share or sell any personal data. It has no internet access, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

## Data on your device

To play your music, Sim Music uses the following on your phone only:

- **Your music files:** titles, artists, albums, folders and album art, read from your device's media library to show and play them.
- **Settings and playback state:** your preferences, accent color, and the last play queue and position (so playback can resume), saved in Sim Music's private app storage.

None of this leaves your device.

## Android Auto

When you use Sim Music in a car through Android Auto, your library and album art are shown on the car screen. This goes directly from your phone to the car; nothing is sent to the developer or any other service.

## Permissions

- **Music and audio:** used to find and play the music files on your device.
- **Notifications:** used to show playback controls while music is playing.
- **Foreground service:** used to keep music playing when the app is in the background.
- **Modify system settings:** used only when you choose "Set as ringtone", and only after you allow it.

Deleting a track always asks for your confirmation, and Android shows its own confirmation before any file is removed.

## Children

Sim Music does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
